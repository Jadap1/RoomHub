Set up a RoomHub Tab5

This installer keeps Wi-Fi credentials in your browser and sends them directly to the Tab5 over USB. They are not uploaded to RoomHub.

1. Install signed firmware

Connect the Tab5 with a USB data cable. The initial installation erases its existing configuration; later releases arrive through RoomHub OTA.
